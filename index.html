The Problem

Visual STEM Content Remains Inaccessible

Worldwide, over 300 million people live with vision impairment or blindness — a figure projected to more than double by 2050. The WHO estimates $411 billion in annual global productivity losses due to vision impairment.

Braille, while essential for text, cannot convey spatial, quantitative, or visual information. Current manual tactile graphics production costs $50–$200 per image with turnaround times of 2–4 weeks, making scalable access economically impossible.
